## Commission Rate Schedule

### Client Referral Commissions

| Period | Rate | Calculation Base | Example (per $1,500 project) |
|--------|------|------------------|------------------------------|
| **Years 1-3** | 10% | Total Project Cost (TPC) | $150 |
| **Year 4+** | 3% | Total Project Cost (TPC) | $45 |

**Cliff Date:** Rate changes on the third anniversary of the first completed project.

### Consultant Referral Commissions

| Period | Rate | Calculation Base | Example (per $1,500 project) |
|--------|------|------------------|------------------------------|
| **All Years** | 1.5% | Allocated Cost (AC) | ~$17 |

**Note:** Allocated Cost is the portion paid to consultants (typically 75% of TPC).

## Payment Timeline

### Standard Payment Process

| Event | Timeline | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Project Completed | Day 0 | Work delivered, accepted by client |
| Invoice Sent | Day 1-5 | Company invoices client |
| Client Payment Due | Day 30 | Standard Net 30 terms |
| Commission Calculated | Day 31-35 | Upon receipt of client payment |
| Commission Processed | Day 35-45 | Payment prepared and queued |
| **Partner Paid** | **Day 45-60** | Within 30 days of client payment |

### Payment Schedule Commitment

**Company commits to pay Partners within 30 days of receiving client payment.**

## Payment Frequency

### Standard Schedule

- **Monthly:** All commissions earned in a calendar month are paid the following month
- **Cutoff:** Last day of the month at 11:59 PM ET
- **Processing:** 1st-15th of the following month
- **Payment:** By the 20th of the following month

### Example Timeline

| Earned | Cutoff | Processed | Paid |
|--------|--------|-----------|------|
| January | Jan 31 | Feb 1-15 | Feb 20 |
| February | Feb 28/29 | Mar 1-15 | Mar 20 |
| March | Mar 31 | Apr 1-15 | Apr 20 |

## Minimum Payout Threshold

### Standard Threshold

| Threshold | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| < $50 | Carried forward to next month |
| ≥ $50 | Paid on regular schedule |

### Requesting Early Payment

Partners may request payment of amounts below $50:
- Email accounting@myco.com
- Subject: "Partner [ID] - Early Payment Request"
- Processing may take additional 5-7 business days

### Year-End Exception

All accumulated commissions are paid by December 31, regardless of minimum.

## Special Situations

### Project Cancellation

If a project is cancelled before client payment:
- No commission is owed
- Partner is notified of status change

### Partial Payment

If client pays partially:
- Commission calculated on amount received
- Additional commission paid when remainder received

### Client Refund

If client receives refund:
- Proportional commission may be reversed
- Partner notified before any clawback
- Disputes handled per Terms and Conditions

### Client Churn

If client becomes inactive (no projects for 12+ months):
- Commission earnings pause
- Resume if client re-engages
- Rate based on original cliff date

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do I start earning?

You start earning when your referred client completes their first project and pays.

### How is the Cliff Date calculated?

The Cliff Date is exactly 3 years after the first project's completion date.

### What if a client skips a year?

You still earn on any projects they complete. The cliff date doesn't change.

### Can I get paid faster?

ACH is the fastest method (2-3 business days after processing). Ensure your bank information is correct.

### What happens to pending commissions if I leave the program?

You continue earning on referrals made before termination, paid per regular schedule.
