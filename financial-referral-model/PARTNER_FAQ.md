# MyCo Partner Program - Frequently Asked Questions

---

## General Questions

### What is the MyCo Partner Program?

The MyCo Partner Program allows you to earn commissions by referring clients and consultants to MyCo. When your referrals engage with MyCo, you earn a percentage of the project value.

### Who can become a partner?

Anyone with a professional network can become a partner. Ideal partners include:
- Business professionals
- Former MyCo clients
- Industry consultants
- Networking groups
- Professional associations

### Is there a cost to join?

No. The partner program is completely free to join.

### Is there a minimum commitment?

No. You can refer as much or as little as you want. There are no quotas or minimums.

## Earnings & Payouts

### How much can I earn?

Your earnings depend on the value and volume of referrals:

| Scenario | 5-Year Earnings |
|----------|-----------------|
| 1 client referral | $1,080 |
| 3 client referrals | $3,240 |
| 5 client referrals | $5,400 |
| 10 client referrals | $10,800 |

*Based on $1,500 TPC, 2 projects/year*

### What are the commission rates?

**Client Referrals:**
- Years 1-3: **10%** of Total Project Cost
- Year 4+: **3%** of Total Project Cost

**Consultant Referrals:**
- All years: **1.5%** of Allocated Cost

### When do I get paid?

You're paid within 30 days of MyCo receiving payment from the client. Typical timeline:
- Project completed → Client invoiced → Client pays (~30 days) → You're paid (~30 days after)

### How do I get paid?

Options include:
- ACH direct deposit (fastest)
- Check
- Wire transfer (for amounts over $5,000)

### What if a project is cancelled?

You only earn on completed, paid projects. If a project is cancelled before payment, no commission is due.

### Are there any caps on earnings?

For standard projects, there are no caps. For enterprise projects (TPC > $10,000), MyCo may negotiate custom terms.

## Making Referrals

### How do I make a referral?

Three easy options:
1. **Share your unique link** - Prospects who use your link are tracked to you
2. **Email introduction** - Introduce your contact to MyCo via email
3. **Register directly** - Email partners@myco.com with referral details

### What information do I need to provide?

At minimum:
- Prospect name
- Company name
- Contact information (email or phone)
- Brief description of their needs (optional but helpful)

### What happens after I make a referral?

1. MyCo reaches out within 24 business hours
2. Discovery call scheduled
3. Proposal created (if fit)
4. Client engages
5. You start earning

### How long do I have to register a referral?

Register referrals before or within 30 days of MyCo's first contact with them. After 30 days, we may not be able to credit you.

### What if someone I know is already talking to MyCo?

If MyCo is already in active discussions with a prospect, we cannot credit a new referral. The first registered referrer gets credit.

## Tracking & Reporting

### How do I track my referrals?

Use the Partner Portal at partners.myco.com to see:
- All your referrals and their status
- Pending and completed earnings
- Payment history
- Lifetime statistics

### What do the referral statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Referral submitted, not yet contacted |
| Qualified | In active sales discussions |
| Proposal | Proposal sent to prospect |
| Active | Client engaged, you're earning |
| Closed | Did not convert |
| Churned | Client no longer active |

### Do I get notified of updates?

Yes. You'll receive email notifications for:
- Referral status changes
- Project completions
- Payments processed
- Monthly summaries

## Taxes & Legal

### Do I need to sign a contract?

Yes. All partners sign a Partner Agreement that outlines terms, rates, and responsibilities.

### Will I receive tax documents?

Yes. If you earn more than $600 in a calendar year, you'll receive a 1099-NEC form by January 31 of the following year.

### Are earnings taxable?

Yes. Referral commissions are considered taxable income. Consult your tax advisor for specific guidance.

### Can I refer clients internationally?

Yes, but payment and tax handling may vary by country. Contact us for international partnership terms.

## Relationship Terms

### How long does my referral relationship last?

Your commission on a client referral lasts as long as that client remains active with MyCo. There's no expiration.

### What if the client stops using MyCo?

If a client becomes inactive (no projects for 12+ months), your earnings pause. If they return, your earnings resume at the current rate.

### Can my rates change?

The rates locked in your Partner Agreement apply for the duration of each referral relationship. However, rates for new referrals may change if MyCo updates the program.

### What if I want to leave the program?

You can leave at any time. However, you'll continue to receive commissions on active referrals made before your departure.

---

## Multiple Referrals

### Can I refer both a client AND a consultant?

Yes! If you refer both the client and a consultant working on that client's project, you earn both commissions.

### What if I refer multiple clients?

Each client referral is tracked separately. You earn independently on each.

### What if two partners claim the same referral?

The partner who registered the referral first receives credit. Documentation (emails, timestamps) may be required.
