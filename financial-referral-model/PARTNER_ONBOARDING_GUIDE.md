## Understanding Your Partnership

### What You're Earning On

You earn commissions when someone you refer becomes a MyCo client and engages our services. Here's the flow:

```
Your Referral → Becomes Client → Uses Services → You Earn
```

### Types of Referrals

| Type | Description | Commission Base |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Client Referral (RO Client)** | You refer someone who hires MyCo | % of Total Project Cost (TPC) |
| **Consultant Referral (RO Consultant)** | You refer talent who joins MyCo | % of their Allocated Cost |

**Note:** Client referrals earn significantly more because they drive direct revenue.

## Your Payout Structure

### Client Referral Rates (RO Client)

| Period | Your Rate | Example per $1,500 project |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Years 1-3 | **10% of TPC** | $150 |
| Year 4+ | **3% of TPC** | $45 |

### Consultant Referral Rates (RO Consultant)

| Period | Your Rate | Example per $1,500 project |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------|
| All Years | **1.5% of Allocated Cost** | ~$17 |

### Key Terms

- **TPC (Total Project Cost):** What the client pays for a project
- **Allocated Cost (AC):** The portion of TPC paid to consultants (75% of TPC)
- **Cliff:** The point where your rate drops (Year 4)

## How to Make Referrals

### Option 1: Share Your Link

Your unique referral link: `myco.com/ref/[YOUR-ID]`

When prospects use this link to inquire, they're automatically tracked to you.

### Option 2: Email Introduction

Send a warm introduction:

```
Subject: Introduction - [Prospect Name] & MyCo

Hi [MyCo Contact],

I'd like to introduce [Prospect Name] from [Company]. 
[Brief description of their needs].

I think MyCo could be a great fit for them.

[Prospect], meet [MyCo Contact]. They help companies like 
yours with [relevant service].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Option 3: Direct Notification

Email partners@myco.com with:
- Prospect name and company
- Contact information
- Brief description of their needs
- How you know them

### What Happens Next

1. **We reach out** within 24 business hours
2. **Discovery call** to understand their needs
3. **Proposal** if there's a fit
4. **Engagement** - they become a client
5. **You earn** on every project

## Getting Paid

### Payment Schedule

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Client completes project | Day 0 |
| Client pays invoice | ~Day 30 |
| Your commission processes | Day 30-45 |
| Payment sent to you | Day 45-60 |

**Standard Payment:** Within 30 days of client payment receipt

### Payment Methods

- **ACH Direct Deposit** (preferred)
- **Check** (add 5-7 days)
- **Wire Transfer** (for amounts >$5,000)

### Tax Information

- You'll receive a **1099-NEC** for earnings over $600/year
- Keep track of your referral expenses for deductions
- Consult your tax advisor for questions

## Best Practices

### DO:

- **Warm introductions** - Personal connections convert better
- **Qualify first** - Make sure they actually need consulting help
- **Stay informed** - Know what MyCo offers so you can speak to it
- **Follow up** - Check in on your referrals' status
- **Refer early** - The sooner you refer, the longer you earn

### DON'T:

- **Cold spam** - Don't mass email your contacts
- **Over-promise** - Let MyCo handle the sales pitch
- **Pressure** - Let prospects decide on their timeline
- **Forget to register** - Always log your referrals

### Ideal Referral Profile

Your best referrals are typically:

- Companies with 50-500 employees
- Facing a specific project or transformation
- Have budget allocated for external help
- Decision-maker is accessible
- Need expertise they don't have in-house
