## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Company operates a consulting and project management services business; and

WHEREAS, Partner desires to refer potential clients and/or consultants to Company in exchange for referral compensation as set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Allocated Cost" or "AC"** means the portion of the Total Project Cost paid to consultants for project work, typically 75% of TPC.

**1.2 "Client Referral"** means a business entity or individual referred by Partner who subsequently enters into a service agreement with Company.

**1.3 "Cliff Date"** means the date three (3) years after the First Project Date for a given Client Referral.

**1.4 "Consultant Referral"** means a consultant or freelancer referred by Partner who subsequently joins Company's talent network and performs compensated work.

**1.5 "First Project Date"** means the date on which the first project for a referred client is completed and invoiced.

**1.6 "Referral"** means either a Client Referral or Consultant Referral.

**1.7 "Total Project Cost" or "TPC"** means the total amount invoiced to a client for a completed project.

---

## ARTICLE 2: REFERRAL PROCESS

**2.1 Registration.** Partner shall register all Referrals with Company via:
- (a) Company's online referral portal; or
- (b) Email to partners@myco.com; or
- (c) Personal introduction with written follow-up.

**2.2 Information Required.** For each Referral, Partner shall provide:
- (a) Name of individual and/or company;
- (b) Contact information (email and/or phone);
- (c) Brief description of potential needs (if known);
- (d) Partner's relationship to the Referral.

**2.3 Timing.** Partner must register a Referral before or within thirty (30) days of Company's first substantive contact with the referred party.

**2.4 Qualification.** Company shall make all determinations regarding qualification, engagement, and acceptance of Referrals at its sole discretion.

---

## ARTICLE 3: COMPENSATION

**3.1 Client Referral Commission.**

| Period | Commission Rate | Calculation Base |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|
| Years 1-3 (before Cliff Date) | 10% | Total Project Cost |
| Year 4+ (after Cliff Date) | 3% | Total Project Cost |

**3.2 Consultant Referral Commission.**

| Period | Commission Rate | Calculation Base |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|
| All Years | 1.5% | Allocated Cost |

**3.3 Commission Examples.**

For a $1,500 TPC project:
- Client Referral (Years 1-3): $150 per project
- Client Referral (Year 4+): $45 per project
- Consultant Referral: ~$17 per project (1.5% of $1,125 AC)

**3.4 Payment Terms.** Company shall pay commissions within thirty (30) days of receiving client payment for completed projects.

**3.5 Payment Method.** Commissions shall be paid via:
- (a) ACH direct deposit (preferred); or
- (b) Check; or
- (c) Wire transfer (for amounts exceeding $5,000).

**3.6 Minimum Threshold.** Company may accumulate commissions and pay when total reaches $50, unless Partner requests earlier payment.

---

## ARTICLE 4: TERM AND TERMINATION

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated by either party.

**4.2 Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement:
- (a) For convenience, with thirty (30) days written notice; or
- (b) Immediately, for material breach by the other party.

**4.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
- (a) Partner shall cease making new Referrals;
- (b) Company shall continue paying commissions on Referrals made prior to termination, subject to the terms herein;
- (c) Partner's access to Company's partner portal shall be revoked.

**4.4 Survival.** Articles 5, 6, 7, and 8 shall survive termination.

**5.2 Non-Disclosure.** Partner shall not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without Company's prior written consent.

**5.3 Exceptions.** This Article does not apply to information that:

- (b) Duration of any client or consultant relationship;

**7.2 Limitation.** Company's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions paid to Partner in the twelve (12) months preceding any claim.

**7.3 Exclusion.** In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.
