## 1. Program Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
The MyCo Referral Program ("Program") rewards individuals and organizations ("Partners") who refer potential clients and consultants to MyCo, Inc. ("Company").

### 1.2 Eligibility
Any individual or entity may apply to become a Partner, subject to Company approval. Employees of Company and their immediate family members are not eligible unless specifically approved.

### 1.3 Enrollment
To participate, you must:
- Complete the Partner Application
- Provide required tax documentation (W-9 or W-8BEN)
- Accept these Terms and Conditions
- Receive written confirmation of acceptance

## 2. Referral Types and Compensation

### 2.1 Client Referrals

A Client Referral occurs when you refer a business or individual who subsequently engages Company for services.

**Compensation Structure:**

| Period | Commission Rate |
|--------|-----------------|
| Years 1-3 | 10% of Total Project Cost (TPC) |
| Year 4+ | 3% of Total Project Cost (TPC) |

The "Cliff" occurs at Year 4, meaning the rate reduces from 10% to 3% on the third anniversary of the first completed project.

### 2.2 Consultant Referrals

A Consultant Referral occurs when you refer a consultant or freelancer who joins Company's talent network and completes compensated work.

**Compensation Structure:**

| Period | Commission Rate |
|--------|-----------------|
| All Years | 1.5% of Allocated Cost (AC) |

Allocated Cost is the portion of project fees paid to consultants (typically 75% of TPC).

## 3. Referral Registration

### 3.1 How to Register
Register referrals via:
- **Online:** Partner portal at partners.myco.com
- **Email:** partners@myco.com
- **Introduction:** Email introduction with "Referral" in subject line

### 3.2 Required Information
- Referred party's name and contact information
- Company name (for client referrals)
- Brief description of needs or expertise
- Your relationship to the referred party

### 3.3 Registration Deadline
Referrals must be registered before or within 30 days of Company's first contact with the referred party.

### 3.4 Credit Determination
- First Partner to register a valid referral receives credit
- Documentation (timestamps, emails) may be required
- Company's determination is final

## 4. Payment Terms

### 4.1 When You're Paid
Commissions are paid within 30 days of Company receiving client payment for completed projects.

### 4.2 How You're Paid
- ACH direct deposit (preferred)
- Check (add 5-7 business days)
- Wire transfer (for amounts over $5,000)

### 4.3 Minimum Payout
Company may accumulate commissions until reaching $50, unless you request earlier payment.

### 4.4 Currency
All payments are in US Dollars.

### 4.5 Disputed Amounts
If you dispute a commission amount, notify Company in writing within 30 days of payment. Company will review and respond within 15 business days.
