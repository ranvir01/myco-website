## FINAL APPROVED PAYOUT STRUCTURE

| Component | Rate | Base |
|-----------|------|------|
| **Consultant Payout** | **75%** | of TPC |
| **RO Client (Years 1-3)** | **10%** | of TPC |
| **RO Client (Year 4+)** | **3%** | of TPC |
| **RO Consultant** | **1.5%** | of Allocated Cost |
| **Cliff Year** | **Year 4** | (after 3 years at high rate) |

## Why These Rates?

### Consultant at 75%
- Frees up 25% margin (vs 15% before)
- Still competitive in the market (industry range: 60-85%)
- Allows meaningful RO payouts

### RO Client at 10%/3%
- High early rate incentivizes referrals
- Lower late rate protects long-term margins
- Partner earns $1,000+ with single referral

### RO Consultant at 1.5%
- Small but meaningful incentive
- RO Client earns 6x more (correct hierarchy)
- Doesn't drain MyCo margin

### Cliff at Year 4
- 3 years of high payouts rewards referrers
- Rate reduction after Year 3 protects margins
- Referrer isn't actively contributing by Year 4+

## Summary

**The payout structure is finalized:**

- **Consultants** get 75% of every project
- **Client referrers** get 10% for Years 1-3, then 3% ongoing
- **Consultant referrers** get 1.5% of the consultant's allocation
- **MyCo** keeps ~14% net margin

This structure:
- Meets the $1,000 partner earnings target
- Maintains proper incentive hierarchy (clients > consultants)
- Provides sustainable MyCo margins
- Protects long-term profitability with cliff structure
