# Partner Earnings Examples
## Real-World Scenarios

---

## How to Read These Examples

All examples use the **Final Approved Payout Structure**:
- **Consultant Payout:** 75% of TPC
- **RO Client (Years 1-3):** 10% of TPC
- **RO Client (Year 4+):** 3% of TPC
- **RO Consultant:** 1.5% of Allocated Cost
- **Cliff:** Year 4

## Key Takeaways

1. **One good referral = $1,000+** over 5 years
2. **Enterprise clients** can 5x your earnings per referral
3. **Volume matters** - more referrals = more income
4. **Long-term relationships** continue paying (at 3%)
5. **Client referrals > Consultant referrals** (by design)
6. **No risk** - even a churned small client earns you something
