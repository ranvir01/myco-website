# 09 — The First 14 Days: Sprint to First Revenue

**Mission:** first paid client(s) inside 14 days, on a tool budget of ~$100–$300/mo and $0 ad spend.
**Who does what:** role assignments in `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`. Numbers logged daily per `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`. Offers and prices quoted ONLY from `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md`.

**The one strategic fact this plan is built on:** cold email domains need **2–4 weeks of warmup** before real volume (per `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md` / deliverability consensus) — so **cold email cannot produce revenue in 14 days**. We set it up on Day 1 so it fires in week 3+, and we make the first money from the four channels that work immediately:

1. **Warm network** (11 people × their phones) — fastest money.
2. **The 8 past clients** — already trust us; reactivate with a specific new AI offer.
3. **Referral partners** — 10 activated in week 1 (`07_REFERRAL_ACTIVATION.md`).
4. **Cold calls** — legal, free, and effective on clean local SMB lists (~20–26% connect rates on local lists with owner numbers vs ~5.4% on generic ones — Cognism/Gong; details in `04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md`).

## 1. Compliance rules baked into every day below (not optional)

**Calls (`04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md` has the full version):**
- **Manual/click-to-dial only. No autodialers, no prerecorded or AI voices** — TCPA applies to how you dial, B2B included; cell phones require prior consent for automated tech, and many SMB "business lines" are the owner's personal cell (courts treat mixed-use cells as protected — *Chennette v. Porch*). Penalties: $500–$1,500/call.
- Call **business numbers**, between **8am–8pm recipient local time** (federal window is 8am–9pm; FL/OK and others cut off at 8pm — we adopt 8pm everywhere so nobody has to remember state lists).
- **Max 3 call attempts per 24h** to the same person on the same matter (FL/OK cap — adopted globally).
- Anyone says any version of "take me off your list" → log them in the shared **Do-Not-Contact tab** immediately; never call again. Identify yourself and MyCo truthfully on every call (TSR misrepresentation rules now cover B2B; penalties up to $53,088/violation).
- True B2B calls are generally DNC-registry-exempt, but if a number looks personal (sole prop, cell), treat it like a consumer line: check tone, ask permission, log consent.

**Email:** nothing cold goes out from **myconsulting.network** — ever. Cold sends only from the warmed lookalike domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC set, ≤30/day/mailbox at steady state, one-click unsubscribe on, **physical mailing address in every footer**, accurate From/subject lines, opt-outs honored same-day (law allows 10 business days; we don't need 10). Keep bounce <2% (verify every list) and spam complaints <0.1%.

**Texts:** person-to-person texts to people who know you (warm network) = fine. **No bulk/cold SMS to prospects** — that needs A2P 10DLC registration and consent we don't have.

## 3. The warm-network script (personal text/DM — the fastest money)

Send from your own phone, personalized first line, one at a time. Never BCC-blast.

> Hey [name] — quick one! I'm working with MyConsulting Network (small consulting team, we help small businesses use AI practically — think "never miss a call again," not robot hype). This month we're doing **free AI Opportunity Audits**: we secretly test a business's phones + website + Google listing, then send a 5-point plan of where money's leaking. Takes them 30 min, costs nothing.
> Do you know a business owner — landscaper, dentist, contractor, lawyer, restaurant owner, anyone — who'd want one? An intro would mean a lot. (And heads up: we pay referrers 10% of project revenue for 3 years if it turns into work — typical referral is worth $1,000+.)

**Rules:** first line must be personal ("how was the wedding?") · if they ARE a business owner, offer them the audit directly · any reply gets a same-day response · a "no" gets a thank-you, not a pitch · log every send in the tracker.

Expected math: 220 personal messages → 30–40% reply on real personal texts → 15–25 real conversations → **5–10 audits + a handful of intros**. This channel alone can hit the Day-7 audit target.

## 5. Service-level rules that make us look bigger than we are

1. **Inbound response:** every inquiry answered within 4 business hours (public promise is 24h — beat it).
2. **Audit SLA:** audit call offered within 48h of request; **5-point plan delivered within 48h of the call** (canonical promise — never miss it; a late free audit kills the whole trust play).
3. **Proposal-within-24h rule:** any audit walkthrough or scoping call where the prospect says "send me pricing" → written proposal (template in `templates/`) in their inbox **within 24 hours**, with the one lost-revenue number from their audit at the top, one recommended option + one cheaper option, and the 7-day founding-client expiry date.
4. **Every proposal gets a follow-up call at 48h.** Proposals don't close themselves.

## 6. Pilot pricing rules (what the team may offer WITHOUT asking a founder)

Full rules in `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §9. Sprint summary:

- **Founding-client pilot (first 10 logos):** 20% off any setup fee **OR** 50% off the first retainer month — never both. Trade, in writing, for: Google review at day 30 (if happy) + case-study rights + 2 warm intros.
- **Floors:** never below **$400** on any project (break-even is $360); never below **$99/mo** on any retainer. Audits are the only free thing.
- **Every discount expires 7 days** after the proposal date — say it in the proposal, enforce it on Day 12.
- No "pay if it works," no spec work, no unpriced "quick favors." Anything outside these lines → founder call.
