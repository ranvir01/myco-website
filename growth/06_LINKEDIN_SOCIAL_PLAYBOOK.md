# 06 — LinkedIn & Social Playbook: 11 People, One Voice, Zero Ad Spend

**The thesis:** MyCo has no ad budget but has 11 real humans. Eleven active LinkedIn profiles posting and commenting is a distribution network most agencies would pay thousands for. This channel warms up every other channel — prospects who get our cold email or call (see `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`, `04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md`) WILL look us up. What they find must make them relax.

**Goal of this channel:** booked **Free AI Opportunity Audits** (the CTA everywhere is `myconsulting.network/free-ai-audit`) plus a credibility layer under all outbound. Expect it to compound over 4–8 weeks, not overnight.

**Time budget per person: 30–45 minutes/day.** More is not better; consistency is everything.

**Ground rules (baked in, non-negotiable):**
1. **Manual only.** No LinkedIn automation tools, scrapers, or auto-connect bots — they violate LinkedIn ToS and get accounts restricted (see `RESEARCH_OUTREACH.md` §6). Everything in this playbook is done by hand.
2. **No fabricated results, clients, or testimonials.** Public claims we may use: Tabletop Village "grew bottom line by over 300%," Blue Landscaping's lead growth (qualitative), 12+ happy clients, 25+ projects, founded 2023, real client names from `01_TARGET_SEGMENTS.md` / the canonical brief. Research stats are fine WITH the source named.
3. **Always disclose the affiliation.** Anywhere any of us recommends MyCo (Facebook group, Nextdoor, Reddit, a comment thread), say "full disclosure — I work at MyCo." FTC endorsement rules require disclosing a material connection, and undisclosed shilling that gets caught burns the brand permanently.
4. **DMs are conversations, not broadcasts.** If a prospect says "not interested," thank them and stop — forever. If a conversation moves to email, CAN-SPAM rules apply from the first email (`03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`).

## 2. Content engine: 4-week calendar, 3 posts/week/person MAX

**Cadence rules:**
- Max 3 posts/week per person, min 1. One post per person per day max. Quality collapses past that, and 11 people x 3 posts = 33 posts/week of team output — plenty.
- **Stagger, don't synchronize:** if all 11 post the same morning it looks botted. The weekly rota (owner: marketing lead, per `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`) assigns each person their 3 days.
- **Everyone engages with teammates' posts within 60 minutes** (a real comment, not "Great post!"). Early engagement is what LinkedIn's feed rewards; 10 teammates is a built-in engagement pod — a legitimate one, because the comments are real.
- **Format rules:** hook line first (people see only ~2 lines before "…see more"), short paragraphs, no more than 1 link and put it in the comments if the post doesn't need it, 3 hashtags max (#smallbusiness + one segment tag + one local tag). Write like you talk. No corporate voice — ever.

**The 4 archetypes (rotate; every person hits each at least once per month):**

| Archetype | What it is | CTA style |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Client-win story** | Real MyCo client, real (public) result, told as a story: situation → what we did → outcome | Soft: "If you want us to look at yours — free audit link in comments" |
| **B. Before/after teardown** | Take a real, anonymized local business situation (missed-call test results, a GBP with 3-year-old photos, a site with no phone number above the fold) and show the fix | "Want the same 10-minute check on your business? DM me 'audit'" |
| **C. Contrarian AI take** | Push back on AI hype OR AI dismissal — honest, specific, from actual client work | None, or a question to drive comments |
| **D. Build-in-public** | What our 11-person team actually did this week: dials made, audits run, what worked, what flopped | None — this archetype builds trust, not leads |

**The 4-week calendar (per person — pick your lane's segment for A and B posts):**

| Week | Post 1 (Mon/Tue) | Post 2 (Wed/Thu) | Post 3 (Fri/Sat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D: "why I joined an 11-person AI consulting team" intro post | C: contrarian take | B: teardown from your segment |
| 2 | A: client-win story (Tabletop Village or Blue Landscaping) | D: week-1 outbound numbers, honestly | C: contrarian take |
| 3 | B: missed-call test teardown (use your real test data from `04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md` §3) | A: client-win or audit-findings story | D: lesson learned |
| 4 | C: contrarian take | B: teardown | A + month recap; re-pin best post to Featured |

## 3. Connections & DMs: value first, audit second, never spam

**Who to connect with (your lane's list — build it manually from `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md` sources):** owners/partners/GMs of businesses in your assigned segment + metro; local chamber-of-commerce members; adjacent non-competitors who talk to your buyers (bookkeepers, insurance agents, commercial realtors — these double as referral partners, see `07_REFERRAL_ACTIVATION.md`); people who engage with local-business content.

**Daily limits (per person — LinkedIn restricts aggressive accounts, and new/low-activity accounts are watched hardest):**

| Action | Daily cap | Weekly cap |
|---|---|---|
| Connection requests | **10** | 50 (LinkedIn's own soft cap is ~100–200/wk; we stay far under) |
| DMs (new threads) | 10–15 | — |
| Withdraw stale invites | — | withdraw any request older than 3 weeks (a pile of pending invites is a spam flag) |

Acceptance rate below ~25%? Your notes are too pitchy — stop and rewrite before sending more.

**Connect-note variants (keep under ~200 characters; free-account note limits are tight — or send WITHOUT a note, which often converts just as well for local connects):**

- V1 (commented first — the best path): "Enjoyed your post about [specific thing] — I work with [segment] businesses here in [metro] on the ops side. Would be glad to connect."
- V2 (local/peer angle): "Hi [Name] — I'm with a small consulting team here in [metro] working with [segment] businesses like yours. No pitch, just building a local network."
- V3 (missed-call test, only if you genuinely ran it): "Hi [Name] — I did a quick phone-answering check on [metro] [segment] businesses this week and yours came up. Happy to share what I found, either way."

**The DM sequence AFTER they accept (manual, spaced, human):**

- **Day 0–1 — thank + real question (NO pitch, NO link):**
  > "Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Curious — [genuine question about their business: 'is this your busy season?' / 'saw you opened the second location — how's the split working?']"
  Then actually converse. Most threads should stay just this: a relationship.
- **Day 3–5 — value drop (specific to THEM, still no ask):**
  > "Was looking at [their business] — two quick things I noticed: your Google listing's photos are a few years old, and when I called Tuesday afternoon it went to voicemail (that's true of 62% of small businesses, so no shame — but 85% of those callers never call back). Happy to send the 3-line fix for the listing if useful — free, no catch."
  Send the fix if they say yes. Full stop. You just became the most useful stranger in their inbox.
- **Day 7–10 — the soft audit offer (one sentence, easy no):**
  > "By the way — we do a free 30-minute AI audit for [segment] businesses: we check phones, Google listing, reviews, and follow-up, and you get a written 5-point plan within 48 hours, yours to keep either way. Want me to grab you a slot this week?"
- **Any "no" / silence after step 3:** one polite close ("All good — the offer stands whenever. Rooting for you either way.") and STOP. No drip #4. They stay a connection, they see your posts, many come back in 60 days.
- **Any "yes":** book it live in the DM (offer two times, calendar link second), then run the handoff per `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §1a and log it per `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`.

## 4. Commenting: the highest-leverage 15 minutes of the day

**Quota: 10 real comments/day per person** on posts by local business owners, segment communities, and adjacent-partner types. Commenting is how strangers with 200 followers get seen — your comment on an active post gets more qualified eyeballs than your own post will for the first month.

**Where to find posts to comment on:** follow 20–30 local owners + your segment's hashtags (#restaurantowner, #lawfirmmarketing, #HVAC, #[yourmetro]smallbusiness); check the chamber of commerce and local BNI members' activity; check who commented on OTHER local-services posts.

**The comment formula (2–4 sentences):** (1) react to their specific point — prove you read it, (2) add one thing from real experience — a stat you can source, a thing we've seen in audits, a tactic, (3) optional question back. **Never** link, **never** pitch, **never** "DM me." Your headline does the selling while your comment does the helping.

Good: "The 6pm-call thing is real — Clio found only 40% of firms answer prospect calls at all. The fix that's worked for firms we've audited isn't more staff, it's texting back every missed call within a minute. Are you seeing most misses after hours or during court blocks?"
Bad: "Great post! We help law firms with AI. DM me!"

## 5. Secondary channels: Facebook groups, Nextdoor, Google Business Profile

These are assigned, not optional — but each gets ONE owner plus volunteers (per `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`), not all 11 people.

### 5a. Facebook local business groups (2–3 people own this)

- Join 5–10 groups each: "[Metro] Small Business Owners," "[Metro] Entrepreneurs," segment groups ("Restaurant Owners Network," "Landscaping Business Owners"). Join as yourself, real profile, MyCo listed as employer.
- **The 2-week rule: first 14 days, zero promotion.** Answer questions, be useful, learn each group's rules (many ban promo entirely or restrict it to a weekly thread — respect that to the letter; one removal = banned from the channel's best group forever).
- **Do:** answer "anyone know someone who does websites/phones/marketing?" threads — recommend MyCo WITH disclosure: "Full disclosure, I work there — but this is literally what we do; happy to answer questions either way." Post pure-value how-tos ("I tested 20 local businesses' phone lines — here's the 5-minute self-check"). Share numbers and templates freely. Reply to every comment.
- **Don't:** drop links in posts (share the link only when someone asks or in a comment if rules allow); DM group members cold (that's how you get reported to admins); post the same content in 5 groups the same day; argue with anyone, ever.
- **The value-post formula (works in every group):** hook with a local, concrete finding → 3–5 genuinely useful steps the reader can do themselves → soft close: "happy to answer questions in comments." No link. When people ask "do you do this?" — THEN the audit link, with disclosure.

### 5b. Nextdoor (1–2 people who actually live in target neighborhoods)

- Claim the free **business page** for MyCo (owner: a founder) — it captures searches and recommendations, but business pages can't post to the main feed (paid Local Deals only — skip those for now).
- Feed presence comes from **personal accounts**: team members who genuinely live there can be helpful neighbors — answer "can anyone recommend someone for a website?" threads. **Always disclose:** "I work at MyConsulting Network and this is what we do — so, biased, but happy to help either way." Undisclosed self-recommendation violates Nextdoor rules and FTC endorsement guidance, and neighbors WILL notice.
- Never create fake accounts or have teammates who don't live there pretend to. Instant ban, deserved.

### 5c. Google Business Profile posts (owner: whoever runs `05_SEO_PLAYBOOK.md`)

- 1 GBP post/week minimum, every week, forever. Types on rotation: (1) audit-offer post ("Free AI Opportunity Audit — 30 min + written 5-point plan," CTA button → `myconsulting.network/free-ai-audit`), (2) tip post (reuse the week's best LinkedIn post, trimmed to ~750 characters), (3) client-win post (public claims only).
- Every post: one real photo or branded graphic (MyCo green), one CTA button, no keyword stuffing.
- This doubles as a local-SEO signal — coordinate with the GBP setup and review-engine work in `05_SEO_PLAYBOOK.md` so posts, categories, and NAP stay consistent.

## 6. Weekly rhythm and scoreboard

**Per person, daily (30–45 min):** 10 comments → engage all teammates' posts → send your 10 connects → work active DM threads → post if it's your rota day.

**Weekly (team, in the Friday review per `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`):**
- Each person reports: posts published, comments made, connects sent/accepted, DM threads opened, **audits booked from social** (tracked with source="LinkedIn"/"FB"/"Nextdoor" in `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`).
- Best-performing post gets dissected in 5 minutes: hook, archetype, why it moved. Winner's format gets reused by 3 other people next week (rewritten, not copied).
- Marketing lead updates next week's posting rota and refreshes the shared swipe file of hooks.

**What good looks like by week 4:** all 11 profiles optimized; ~120 team posts live; 300–500 new relevant connections across the team; 3–5 audits/week sourced or assisted by social; every cold-call prospect who Googles us finds 11 consistent, human, active profiles instead of a ghost town.
