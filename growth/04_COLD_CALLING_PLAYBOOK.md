# 04 — Cold Calling Playbook: Dials, Door-Knocks, and Booking the Audit

**Who this is for:** every MyCo team member with a phone and 2 hours a day. Cold calling is our fastest channel — email takes 3 weeks of domain warmup (see `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`); a phone works in the next 10 minutes.

**The only goal of a cold call:** book a **Free AI Opportunity Audit** (30-minute call + written 5-point action plan delivered within 48 hours — see `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §1). You are not selling a website or a chatbot on this call. You are selling a free 30-minute conversation. That's it.

Lead lists come from `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`. Log every dial per `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`. Segment picks and pain points come from `01_TARGET_SEGMENTS.md`.

## 1. Legal ground rules — read before your first dial (not optional)

TCPA penalties are $500 per call, $1,500 if willful, and plaintiffs' firms make a living on this. FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule penalties now run up to **$53,088 per violation**, and the March 2024 TSR amendment extended fraud/misrepresentation rules and recordkeeping to B2B calls. The rules below are baked into everything else in this playbook. Follow them exactly.

### The hard rules (print, tape to monitor)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | **Hand-dial or click-to-dial only. No autodialers, no robocalls, no AI voice, no ringless voicemail. Ever.** | TCPA bans automated dialing/prerecorded/AI voice to cell phones without prior express consent — B2B is NOT exempt. Many SMB "business numbers" are the owner's personal cell (courts treat mixed-use cells as protected — *Chennette v. Porch*, 9th Cir.). A human pressing a button per call is the safe lane. |
| 2 | **Call the BUSINESS line** (the number on their Google listing / website). Only call a direct cell if it's publicly listed as the business number. | Business landlines are lowest-risk. Personal cells found through data brokers are how you end up in a lawsuit. |
| 3 | **Call only 8:00am–9:00pm in the PROSPECT'S local time zone.** We set a tighter internal window: **9:00am–8:00pm prospect local.** | Federal telemarketing window is 8am–9pm local. Several states are stricter (FL, OK: 8am–8pm). Our internal window keeps us legal everywhere without a state lookup per call. |
| 4 | **Max 3 call attempts to the same business in any 24 hours; max 6 attempts total over 2 weeks, then park for 90 days.** | FL and OK cap at 3 calls per 24h on the same matter. We apply that cap nationally so nobody has to remember which state is which. |
| 5 | **"Take me off your list" = immediate, permanent internal DNC.** Say "Absolutely — you won't hear from us again. Sorry to bother you," hang up, and mark DNC in the tracker before your next dial. Never argue, never call back, never email instead. | Honoring do-not-call requests immediately is both the law's spirit and basic decency. Our internal DNC list is checked before every list upload (see `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`). |
| 6 | **Identify yourself and MyCo honestly in the first sentence.** Real name, "MyConsulting Network," and if asked, why you're calling and how you got the number ("your number is on your Google listing"). | TSR misrepresentation rules now cover B2B. No fake "survey" pretexts, no pretending to be a customer. |
| 7 | **Do not record calls.** No call-recording apps, no "for training purposes." | Twelve states (CA, WA, FL, and others) require all-party consent. Not worth it. Take notes instead. |
| 8 | **Never text a prospect without asking first.** On a live call: "Want me to text you the confirmation?" — a yes is consent, log it. No cold texts, ever. | Texts to cells fall under TCPA consent rules. A logged verbal yes on a call you initiated by hand is our standard. |
| 9 | **Extra caution states:** FL, OK, WA, MD (mini-TCPAs, some with no B2B exemption). In these states: business landlines only, never a cell, and stick hard to rules 3–4. If the only number for a FL/OK/WA/MD lead is a cell, skip it — email them instead via `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`. | State mini-TCPAs allow private lawsuits and several don't carve out B2B. |
| 10 | **Sole proprietors = consumers.** If the "business" is one person operating under their own name at what's clearly a personal number, treat them like a consumer: check the National DNC Registry posture with a founder before that sub-list goes live (registry scrub every 31 days if we ever do it). Default: leave sole-prop personal numbers off calling lists entirely. | True B2B solicitation is generally DNC-exempt, but sole props at personal numbers are the gray zone that isn't worth the risk. |

**Who owns compliance:** the day's calling lead (see `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`) confirms the list was DNC-checked and time-zone-tagged before the block starts. If you're not sure a number is safe, don't dial it — flag it.

## 2. The math: why your quota is your quota

Benchmarks (Gong 300M-call dataset + SMB caller data, via `RESEARCH_OUTREACH.md`):

- Dial-to-connect: ~5.4% on junk lists, **~20–26% on clean local SMB lists with the owner's business number** — this is why list quality (see `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`) matters more than talent.
- Meetings booked: **~2.3–2.5% of dials ≈ 1 booked audit per ~40 dials** at competent execution.
- Best days: **Tuesday and Thursday** skew highest. Worst: Monday morning, Friday afternoon.

**Your daily quota (per caller, per calling day):**

| Metric | Daily target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dials | **50** | ~2 hours of focused hand-dialing (20–30 dials/hour incl. logging) |
| Conversations (decision-maker) | 8–12 | 15–25% connect on a clean list |
| Audits booked | **1** | 40 dials/meeting math. Some days 0, some days 3. Weekly is what counts. |
| Weekly (4 calling days) | 200 dials → 4–5 audits | The team-wide rollup lives in `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md` |

If you did 50 dials and booked nothing, you did your job — the math pays weekly, not daily. If you did 20 dials and booked nothing, you didn't do your job.

**Best calling windows by segment** (all within the 9am–8pm internal window, prospect local time):

- **Home & field services:** 8:30–9:30am is gold (owner in the truck, before jobs start) — but our internal window starts at 9:00, so hit **9:00–10:00am** and **4:30–6:00pm** (back from jobs).
- **Law / professional services:** 8:30–10:00am or 4:00–5:30pm → use **9:00–10:00am** and **4:00–5:30pm**. Never 12–1:30pm.
- **Restaurants:** **2:00–4:30pm ONLY** (between lunch and dinner rush). Calling a restaurant at 12:15pm or 6:30pm burns the lead and marks you as clueless.

## 3. Pre-block prep: the missed-call test (do this first, it writes your opener for you)

Before each calling block, spend 15 minutes running the **missed-call test** on the day's list:

1. Take today's 50 leads. During their business hours, call each business's main line **once** (this counts as attempt #1 of your 3-per-24h cap — log it).
2. Three outcomes:
   - **No answer / voicemail:** GOLD. Log "MC-FAIL." When you call back in the same block or later that day (attempt #2), your opener is now a live demo: *"I called you at 2:10 this afternoon and got voicemail — that's actually exactly why I'm calling."*
   - **Answered by a human after 6+ rings or a hold:** log "MC-SLOW." Still usable: *"Took about 45 seconds to reach a person — most callers give up around 25."*
   - **Answered fast and well:** log "MC-PASS." Pivot your pitch away from missed calls to their next pain (reviews, quotes, website — check their GBP and site per the audit inspection checklist in `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §1a).
3. The stats that make this land (memorize, cite the source when the owner pushes back):
   - **62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered** (411 Locals study).
   - **85% of callers who hit voicemail never call back** (Numa business-phone data).
   - Missed calls cost the average SMB **~$126K/yr** (Aira missed-call data).
   - Law firms: only **40% answer a new-client call**; **64% of prospects get zero follow-up** (Clio Legal Trends Report).
   - Restaurants: up to **80% of calls missed at peak**; **69% of diners give up on a restaurant that doesn't answer** (Slang.ai).

This test is our single best trick: **we don't claim they miss calls — we prove it, timestamped, on their own phone line.**

## 4. Openers: first 10 seconds decide everything

Gong's 90k-call opener data (see `RESEARCH_OUTREACH.md` §4): "How've you been?" succeeds at 10.0% (6.6x baseline); stating "the reason for my call is…" lifts success 2.1x; **"Did I catch you at a bad time?" performs ~40% WORSE than baseline — never use it.** Permission works when it's confident and specific, not apologetic.

Pick ONE of these three and say it exactly. Slow down. Smile (it's audible). Say their name.

**Opener A — the familiarity pattern-interrupt (default):**
> "Hi [Name], it's [your first name] from MyConsulting Network — how've you been?"
> *(pause — they'll say "…fine, who is this?")*
> "Totally fair — we haven't spoken. The reason for my call is: [one-sentence segment hook from §5]. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and if it's useless you hang up on me?"

**Opener B — the honest cold-call (works on no-nonsense owners):**
> "Hi [Name], [your first name] with MyConsulting Network. I'll be straight with you — this is a cold call. You can hang up now, or give me 30 seconds and then decide. Fair?"
> *(~80% say "go ahead" — people reward honesty and a clear exit)*

**Opener C — the missed-call demo (use whenever the pre-block test hit MC-FAIL — strongest opener we have):**
> "Hi [Name], [your first name] from MyConsulting Network. Quick heads-up more than a sales pitch: I called your main line at [time] today and got voicemail. Studies say 85% of callers who hit voicemail never call back — I was probably one of about a dozen today. Got 30 seconds for what we do about that?"

Rules for all three: your name and "MyConsulting Network" in sentence one (legal rule #6). One breath, then STOP TALKING and let them respond. Never open with "How are you today?!" in a chipper telemarketer voice — that's the pattern you're interrupting.

## 5. Segment scripts (top 3 segments, full branching)

Each script has four branches: **(G)** gatekeeper answers, **(O)** owner answers, **(V)** voicemail, **(B)** booking close. Proof points are real MyCo clients only (per `CANONICAL_BRIEF` — never invent clients or numbers). Full segment intel: `01_TARGET_SEGMENTS.md`.

### 5.1 Home & Field Services (landscaping, HVAC, contractors, cleaning)

**Hook:** missed calls while on job sites = jobs going to the next Google result. Proof client: **Blue Landscaping** (website + SEO work that turned into measurable customer growth — qualitative only, no invented numbers).

**(G) Gatekeeper (office manager/spouse answers):**
> "Hi, this is [name] from MyConsulting Network — is [owner name] around, or is he/she out on jobs right now?"
> - If "out on jobs": "That's actually related to why I'm calling — when he's out, what happens to the phones? …Gotcha. When's he usually reachable, start of day or end of day?" *(Log the answer — that's your callback window. Then:)* "Could you let him know [name] from MyConsulting Network will try him around [time]? It's about the calls that come in while everyone's in the field."
> - If "what's this about?": "We work with home-service companies — we did the website work for Blue Landscaping, a landscaping company — and I wanted 30 seconds with [owner] about calls that get missed during job hours. Is that something you handle, actually?" *(Office managers often own this pain — pitch THEM the audit if they engage.)*

**(O) Owner answers:**
> [Opener A or C] →
> "The reason for my call: we're MyConsulting Network — we work with home-service businesses like Blue Landscaping on their websites and lead follow-up. > *(If MC-FAIL from the test:)* "And honestly — I tested it. I called your line at [time] today and got voicemail."

## 7. Booking the audit (the close)

The moment they say anything resembling yes, stop selling and book:

1. **Alternative-choice close:** "I can do tomorrow at 10:30 or Thursday at 3 — which is better?" (Never "when works for you?" — it invites "I'll get back to you.")
2. **Book it live:** get it on the calendar while on the phone. Confirm their email: "What's the best email for the invite? …And that's the one you actually check?"
3. **Text confirmation — ask first:** "Want me to text you the confirmation and a reminder the morning of?" A yes = consent; log "TXT-OK" in the tracker (legal rule #8). No yes, no text.
4. **Set the frame:** "It's 30 minutes, on the phone or video, whatever you prefer. Before the call we'll look at your website, Google listing, and phone setup ourselves, so we're not wasting your time. Within 48 hours after, you get a written 5-point plan. Nothing to prepare."
5. **Micro-commitment:** "If something blows up and you can't make it, just reply to the invite — but I'll hold the prep work to that slot, so give me a heads-up. Deal?" (Cuts no-shows sharply.)
6. **Hand off:** the audit call itself runs on the script in `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §1b. Whoever booked it does the pre-call inspection checklist (§1a) or hands it to the audit owner per `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` — same day, while it's hot.

## 8. Post-call logging: no log, no dial counted

Log EVERY dial within 60 seconds of hanging up, in the tracker defined in `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`. Fields:

- **Business / contact / number / segment / state** (state matters — rules 3, 4, 9)
- **Attempt #** (this 24h and total — enforces the 3-per-24h / 6-total caps)
- **Missed-call test result:** MC-FAIL / MC-SLOW / MC-PASS
- **Disposition (pick one):** `NO-ANSWER` · `VM-LEFT` · `GATEKEEPER` · `CONVO-NO` · `CONVO-FOLLOWUP` (with the date THEY gave you) · `AUDIT-BOOKED` · `DNC` · `BAD-NUMBER`
- **TXT-OK?** yes/no (texting consent — only after a live yes)
- **One line of intel:** what they said, who the decision-maker is, best time to reach. Future-you (or a teammate) needs this.

`DNC` entries sync to the master suppression list the same day — that list is checked before every new list goes live and covers calls AND cold email (someone who said "never call me" should not get a cold email Tuesday; see `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md` and `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`).

**End-of-block ritual (5 min):** tally dials / convos / bookings into the daily scoreboard, flag your best call of the day in the team channel (we steal each other's lines — see `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`).

## 9. The walk-in variant: door-knocking for restaurants, retail, and local shops

Eleven people means we can do what no lead-gen SaaS can: **show up.** Walk-ins convert far better than calls for restaurants and storefront retail because the owner can see we're real, local, and human. Pair walk-ins with the same-day missed-call test for the strongest one-two punch we have.

**Targeting & timing:**
- Restaurants: **2:00–4:30pm** (between rushes) — NEVER during service.
- Retail / entertainment (game stores, salons, gyms): weekday **10:00am–12:00pm** (quiet floor time).
- Plan a walking route of 8–12 businesses within a few blocks (build it from the Google Maps list in `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`). Two people max per route — one talks, one notes. Never send three; it reads as a shakedown.
- Dress: clean casual (MyCo green if we have shirts). Bring: one-pagers, phone, and a calendar you can book on the spot.
- **Run the missed-call test on the route businesses that morning** — walking in with "I called you at 11:40 and got voicemail" is devastatingly effective.

**The 60-second walk-in script:**
> *(To whoever's at the counter, relaxed, not salesy:)*
> "Hey — is the owner or manager in? …I'm [name] with MyConsulting Network, we're a local consulting team."
>
> *(When the owner/manager appears — 60 seconds, then shut up:)*
> "Hi [name if known], I'm [your name] — we're MyConsulting Network, a local team that helps small businesses with the boring stuff: phones, Google reviews, websites. Two things and I'm gone:
> One — I called your line this morning at [time] and [got voicemail / waited a while]. Most businesses miss more than half their calls, and 85% of those callers never call back — for a place like this that's bookings and orders going down the street.
> Two — we do a free 30-minute audit: we look at your phones, your Google listing, and your reviews, and you get a written 5-point plan within 48 hours. Free, no strings — it's how we earn a shot at working together. We did marketing strategy for Tabletop Village here in town and they grew the bottom line by over 300%.
> Can I grab 30 minutes on your calendar this week — or should I leave this and swing back [day]?"
>
> *(Whatever they say, leave the one-pager, get a name, and log it like a call.)*

**If the owner isn't in:** "No worries — who should I ask for, and when are they usually around?" Leave the one-pager with: "Would you put this where [owner] will actually see it? Tell them the missed-call thing on it is real — we tested their line this morning." Log name + return window; one return visit max, then switch to phone/email.

**Leave-behind one-pager — required contents (single sheet, big type; build from `templates/` if the walk-in one-pager exists there, otherwise print from this spec):**
- [ ] Header: MyCo logo + "AI-powered consulting for small businesses" + "Local team · Fraction of agency prices"
- [ ] The hook stat, huge: "62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered. 85% of those callers never call back."
- [ ] Handwritten field: "We called your line at ____ on ____ and got: [ ] voicemail [ ] long hold [ ] a great pickup" — **fill it in by hand in front of them** (this is the whole trick)
- [ ] The offer, 3 lines: Free AI Opportunity Audit → 30 minutes → written 5-point plan in 48 hours → no obligation
- [ ] 3 proof logos/names: Tabletop Village · Blue Landscaping · Goldstein & Company ("Trusted by 12+ growing businesses")
- [ ] Starting prices (builds trust): "Phone/chat system from $750 + $150/mo · Websites from $1,500 · Automations from $1,000"
- [ ] QR code → myconsulting.network/free-ai-audit + phone number + info@myconsulting.network
- [ ] Footer: real business mailing address (same one used in `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md` for CAN-SPAM)

**Walk-in quota (for team members on route duty):** 10 doors per outing, 2 outings/week → a booked audit per ~15–20 doors is the early expectation. Log every door like a dial (`WALK-IN` flag in the tracker).

## 10. Print-and-tape daily checklist

**Before the block:**
- [ ] 50 leads pulled, DNC-suppressed, time-zone-tagged (list owner confirms — `08_LEAD_LIST_BUILDING.md`)
- [ ] Missed-call test run on today's list; MC-FAIL leads moved to top
- [ ] Segment script + objection table open; calendar open with 2 bookable slots picked
- [ ] Clock check: every lead inside 9am–8pm THEIR local time

**During:**
- [ ] Hand-dial only · name + MyCo in sentence one · no recording · no unconsented texts
- [ ] Log within 60 seconds of every hangup · DNC marked before the next dial
- [ ] 50 dials or the block isn't done

**After:**
- [ ] Scoreboard updated (`11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`) · DNC synced to master suppression
- [ ] Booked audits handed off same-day with intel notes (`02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §1a prep)
- [ ] Best line of the day posted in team channel
