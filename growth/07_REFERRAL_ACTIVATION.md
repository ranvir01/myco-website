# 07 — Referral Activation: Turning the Finished Program Into a Live Channel

**Owner:** Partnerships owner (a founder for the first 30 days — see `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`). **Siblings:** the pitch lands people into the funnel defined in `00_GTM_MASTER_PLAN.md` · offers referred clients buy are in `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` · sprint timing (partners pitched Days 3–4) in `09_FIRST_14_DAYS_SPRINT.md` · every referral deal logged per `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md` · program legal/mechanics source docs live in `financial-referral-model/`.

**The situation:** the referral program was finalized January 2026 and fully documented (`financial-referral-model/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`, `REFERRAL_TERMS.md`, `PARTNER_AGREEMENT_TEMPLATE.md`, `PAYOUT_SCHEDULE.md`) — and then nobody was ever recruited into it. This playbook turns a finished spreadsheet into a working channel **this week**. Referred leads close at 2–3x cold rates and a referred $1,500 project costs us $150 in year-1 payout — cheaper customer acquisition than any paid channel we could buy.

## 1. The economics (memorize the four bold numbers)

| Who refers | What they earn | Per $1,500 project |
|---|---|---|
| **Client-referrer** (anyone who intros a business that becomes a client) | **10% of project revenue, years 1–3**, then **3% ongoing** from the 3rd anniversary | **$150** (yrs 1–3) / $45 (yr 4+) |
| **Consultant-referrer** (refers a consultant into the network) | 1.5% of that consultant's allocated cost (75% of TPC), all years | ~$17 per project they deliver |

The pitch math (basis: typical client = $1,500/project, 2 projects/year — the canonical model assumption):

- **$300/year** in years 1–3, $90/year after → **$1,080 per referred client over 5 years**; $1,530 over 10.
- Scale: 1 client = $1,080 · 3 = $3,240 · 5 = $5,400 · 10 = **$10,800** (five-year totals).
- Internal Monte Carlo: expected value **~$1,659 per referral**, **~70% probability a referral exceeds $1,000**. (Say "typical referral is worth $1,000+" — that's the honest, defensible headline.)
- **Paid within 30 days** of MyCo receiving the client's payment. Monthly statements. Free to join, no quotas, no caps, non-exclusive, rates locked for the life of each referral.

Why this is safe for MyCo: the 10% comes out of our 25% gross; we still net ~13.9% on referred network projects and far more on in-house-delivered retainers. It is why the discount ceilings in `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md` §9 exist — never stack a founding-client discount AND a referral payout without founder sign-off.

## 3. The 60-second partner pitch (say it, don't send it)

Use on calls, coffees, and walk-ins. Conversational, not read.

> "Quick thing that might genuinely be worth money to you. You talk to small-business owners all day, and I'd bet they ask you about AI — missed calls, websites, automating the paperwork. That's exactly what we do: we're MyConsulting Network, a local team that does AI-powered consulting for small businesses — phone systems that text back every missed call, automation, websites — at a fraction of agency prices.
>
> Here's the offer: when you intro us to a business owner and it turns into work, **you earn 10% of everything they spend with us for three years, then 3% for as long as they stay a client**. A typical client works out to about **$1,080 to you over five years** — so ten intros is a five-figure thing, not a gift card. We pay **within 30 days of every client payment**, you get a monthly statement, it's free to join, no quotas, no caps.
>
> And your name stays safe: every intro starts with a **free AI Opportunity Audit** — a 30-minute call and a written 5-point plan, no obligation — so you're never putting your reputation on a hard sell.
>
> Want me to send the one-pager and your referral link?"

**Objections:**
- *"Is this an MLM thing?"* — "No downlines, no buying in, nothing to sell. You make an intro; if it becomes paid work, you get a cut of that client's spend. That's the whole program."
- *"How do I know you'll pay?"* — "Written agreement, rates locked for the life of each referral, monthly statement, paid within 30 days of each client payment. First payout usually lands 4–6 weeks after your intro closes."
- *"What would I even say?"* — "Nothing salesy. 'These folks do a free audit that shows where your phones and website are leaking money — worth 30 minutes.' We take it from there and you're cc'd on the thank-you."
- *"My clients are tiny."* — "Perfect. Our sweet spot is 2–25 employee local businesses — landscapers, law firms, dentists, restaurants. If they live by their phone and their Google reviews, they're a fit."

## 8. Professional-rules table (respect these or lose the partner AND the reputation)

| Partner type | Rule to respect | What we offer instead |
|---|---|---|
| Attorneys | Ethics rules generally bar paying lawyers for referrals (ABA Model Rule 7.2(b) analogs) | Refer-for-free + we refer clients needing counsel back; or commission redirected as a **discount to the referred client** |
| CPAs | AICPA Code permits referral fees **with written client disclosure** | Provide a 1-line disclosure template with the agreement |
| Bank employees | Internal policies usually bar personal fees | Value swap: reciprocal referrals, co-hosted workshop |
| Anyone | W-9 + 1099-NEC >$600/yr; they are independent contractors, never agents of MyCo | Baked into onboarding step 3 |

Every partner gets the same sentence: *"If your professional rules limit referral fees, tell us — we'll route your commission as a client discount or a reciprocal arrangement instead."* A partner who trusts our compliance instinct refers more.

## 9. Tracking + payout discipline (the channel lives or dies here)

**The iron rule: never pay late. A late $150 payout kills a $10,800 partner.** The program's reputation IS the channel — partners talk to each other.

**Who owns it:** Ops/Finance seat (see `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`) runs the ledger; a founder approves every payout batch.

**The monthly cycle (calendar it now, recurring):**
- **Every close:** the deal row in `templates/pipeline-tracker.csv` carries `referral_partner_id` — no ID, no commission, which is why registration-at-intro matters.
- **1st–5th:** reconcile last month's client payments against `financial-referral-model/referral-tracking-template.csv`; compute commissions with `payout-calculator-template.csv` (10% / 3% / 1.5% per the master CSV rates — never hand-compute).
- **By the 20th:** statements out to every active partner (template below) and payouts sent — ACH preferred, check ok, wire >$5,000. Hard ceiling: **within 30 days of MyCo receiving the client payment**, whichever is sooner.
- **$50 minimum payout** — below it, the balance accrues and the statement says so explicitly ("$34 carried forward — pays out with your next referral activity"). **Year-end sweep: all accrued balances paid by Dec 31** regardless of the minimum.
- **January:** 1099-NEC to every partner paid >$600.

**Monthly statement template (email body, 2 minutes per partner):**

```
Subject: MyCo partner statement — [Month YYYY]

Hi [name],

Your referral statement for [Month]:

Referral            Client project   Client paid    Your rate   Your commission
[Biz A / REF-2026-004]  $1,500 (Sprint)   [date]         10%         $150.00
[Biz A / REF-2026-004]  $150 (retainer)   [date]         10%         $15.00

This month:      $165.00
Carried forward: $0.00
PAID [date] via ACH ending [1234]
Lifetime earnings: $[X]  ·  Active referred clients: [N]

Every payout lands within 30 days of the client's payment. Questions →
reply here. Thanks for the trust — it's the best lead we get.

[Founder] · MyConsulting Network · info@myconsulting.network
[physical mailing address]
```

**Earnings-pause rule (from `REFERRAL_TERMS.md`):** if a referred client goes inactive 12+ months, commission pauses and resumes if they return — the statement must show this status, never just silently drop a line.
