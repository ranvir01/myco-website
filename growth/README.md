## The strategy in one paragraph

MyCo sells **AI-powered consulting for small businesses**: practical AI (websites that convert, automation that answers every call and follows up with every lead) plus a dedicated human project manager, at a fraction of agency prices. The front door is always the **Free AI Opportunity Audit** (30-minute call + written 5-point action plan in 48 hours). The wedge sale is the **front-desk rescue stack** — the AI Receptionist & Chatbot at $750 setup + $150/mo, sold with one number: *62% of small-business calls go unanswered and 85% of callers who hit voicemail never call back* (411 Locals; Numa). We land small, prove ROI in the client's own dashboard, then expand into Local SEO ($500/mo), Workflow Automation Sprints ($1,000), AI Growth Websites ($1,500), and the AI Marketing Engine ($750 + $400/mo). Five channels feed the funnel — cold email, cold calls, LinkedIn, referral partners, SEO — with retainers compounding under project revenue until $10k months are the floor, not the ceiling. First targets: **home & field services** and **professional services (law-led)**.

## How to use this suite

1. **Everyone reads `09_FIRST_14_DAYS_SPRINT.md` first.** That is the live sprint plan — it tells you what to do *today*.
2. **Then each pod reads its own playbook** (pod assignments are in `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`). Don't try to read all twelve documents; own yours cold.
3. **Scripts and copy live in `templates/`** — copy/paste from there, never improvise compliance-sensitive lines (email footers, opt-out language, call openers).
4. **Numbers go in the tracker every day** (`11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`). If it isn't logged, it didn't happen.
5. When two documents disagree, `00_GTM_MASTER_PLAN.md` wins on strategy and pricing; the channel playbook wins on tactics.

## Compliance non-negotiables (memorize)

These are baked into each playbook, but they apply to every human on this team, always:

- **Email:** real From/Reply-To, no deceptive subjects, **physical postal address in every email**, working opt-out in every email, **opt-outs honored within 10 business days** (we do it same day), one-click unsubscribe headers on, spam-complaint target **<0.1%**, never send cold from `myconsulting.network` (secondary domains only, SPF/DKIM/DMARC on each). CAN-SPAM fines run up to **$53,088 per email** (FTC).
- **Calls:** **hand-dial only** — no autodialers, no AI/prerecorded voice (TCPA; many SMB "business" numbers are personal cells). Call **8am–9pm prospect-local time** only; **8am–8pm and max 3 attempts/24h in FL and OK**. Anyone who says "don't call me" goes on our internal do-not-contact list immediately and forever. Scrub sole proprietors against the National DNC Registry every 31 days.
- **AI delivery:** no unsupervised bot ever gives policy, pricing, medical, or legal answers (Moffatt v. Air Canada made companies liable for chatbot answers). No patient data without a BAA and HIPAA-compliant vendors. All client SMS goes through A2P 10DLC-registered numbers.
- **Claims:** no fabricated testimonials, stats, or guarantees. Use only the public proof points listed in `01_TARGET_SEGMENTS.md`.
