## 1. Positioning

**"AI-powered consulting for small businesses."**

MyCo helps small businesses grow with AI: websites that convert, automation that answers every call and follows up with every lead, and practical consulting that pays for itself. Fraction of agency prices. A dedicated project manager on every engagement. Done in weeks, not months.

Why this wins right now:

- The market moved to us: **~68% of US small businesses already use AI** in some capacity (US Chamber/Teneo 2025), SMB AI investment climbed 36% → 42% → 57% from 2023–2025 (Verizon State of Small Business), and **62% of AI-using SMBs plan to increase AI spend** in the next 12 months. We are not evangelizing; we are helping people buy something they already want.
- The incumbents are mispriced for our buyer: full-service AI agencies charge $4,000–$12,000/mo (Digital Agency Network). Point tools (Podium/Birdeye at $289–$589/mo/location, Slang.ai at $399–$600/mo) are software without a human. MyCo sits in the gap: **productized outcomes at $150–$500/mo and $1,000–$1,500 projects, with a human PM** — the tier where a junior-heavy network wins on volume (RESEARCH_SMB_MARKET §5).
- Our delivery model scales: consultants earn 75% of project cost, so delivery capacity grows without payroll, and MyCo nets ~13.9% on brokered work plus full margin on productized retainers the internal team configures.

What we say in one breath: *"We're MyCo — AI-powered consulting for small businesses. We make sure you never miss a call or a lead again, and we do it for less per month than one lost job costs you."*

## 2. The offer ladder (wedge → land → expand)

Every prospect enters through the same door and climbs the same ladder. Canonical names and prices — never quote anything else (details and proposal mechanics: `02_OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md`).

### Step 0 — The door: Free AI Opportunity Audit
30-minute call + a written 5-point action plan showing exactly where AI saves the business time and money, delivered within 48 hours. No obligation. This is the CTA on every email, call, DM, page, and referral ask. A pain-quantifying free audit is the dominant top-of-funnel motion in this market (RESEARCH_SMB_MARKET §4), it costs us ~1 junior-hour, and it produces the killer artifact: **the prospect's own lost-revenue number** ("we called your line twice Tuesday — voicemail both times; at your average job size that's $X/month walking away"). Anchoring to a single lost-revenue number outperforms every feature list.

### Step 1 — The wedge: front-desk rescue
**AI Receptionist & Chatbot — $750 setup + $150/mo.** 24/7 website chat + missed-call text-back + FAQ answers + appointment booking. Why this leads:
- The pain is universal and quantified: **62% of SMB calls go unanswered** (411 Locals) and **85% of voicemail-hitters never call back** (Numa). Missed calls cost an average SMB ~$126K/yr (Aira).
- It's the research-identified sweet spot: the "front-desk rescue" stack (missed-call text-back + AI receptionist + reviews + reminders) for phone-based local businesses, sold at the $150–$500/mo productized tier — configuration on real vendor infrastructure, junior-friendly, hours to deploy (RESEARCH_SMB_MARKET §5).
- At $150/mo we undercut the de-facto reseller standard of $297/mo and category tools at $289–$600/mo, while our $750 setup captures cash up front.
- One recovered job/case/booking pays for months of service. That's the whole pitch.

### Step 2 — Land-and-expand (in typical order)
1. **Local SEO & AI Visibility — from $500/mo** (GBP, reviews engine, local rankings, AI-search visibility). Natural month-2 upsell once the receptionist dashboard shows recovered calls; $500–$1,000/mo is the most common SMB SEO band (SE Ranking).
2. **Workflow Automation Sprint — from $1,000** (quotes, invoicing, scheduling, follow-up).
3. **AI Growth Website — from $1,500, ~2-week delivery.**
4. **AI Marketing Engine — $750 setup + $400/mo** (content + email + CRM + social).
5. **Custom Projects & Fractional Consulting — $1,500–$5,000, scoped free** (dashboards, custom software, feasibility, business plans, cloud).

Rules of the ladder:
- Never open with the website. Open with the wedge; the website sale is 3x easier after we've texted back their missed calls for a month.
- Prove ROI in the client's own dashboard (calls recovered, texts sent, bookings made) — pilot-then-expand is the documented pattern that precedes bigger systems (RESEARCH_SMB_MARKET §4).
- Floor: no engagement under $400; target every deal at $1,000+ total first-year value.

## 3. Revenue math: the road to $10k/mo

Model assumptions (planning numbers — the two close/show rates are our assumptions, everything else is benchmarked):
- Booked audit → held: ~70% show rate (confirmations + reminders per `11_METRICS_AND_TRACKING.md`).
- Held audit → paying client: 25–30% (we bring their own missed-call data to the call; adjust after 30 days of real data).
- Retainer retention: 95%/mo planning figure.

### The 90-day ramp

| | Month 1 (ramp) | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audits held | ~12–15 | ~28–32 | ~35–40 |
| New deals closed | 4 | 8 | 10 |
| — AI Receptionist ($750 + $150/mo) | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| — AI Growth Website ($1,500) | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| — Workflow Sprint ($1,000) | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| — Local SEO ($500/mo) | — | 1 | 1 |
| — AI Marketing Engine ($750 + $400/mo) | — | — | 1 |
| One-time cash (setups + projects) | $4,000 | $7,000 | $8,500 |
| MRR billed in month | ~$300 | ~$1,400 | ~$3,050 |
| **Total month revenue** | **~$4,300** | **~$8,400** | **~$10,000–11,500** |
| Exit MRR | $300 | $1,400 | $3,050 |

Arithmetic check, month 3: setups 5 × $750 + 1 × $750 = $4,500; projects 2 × $1,500 + 1 × $1,000 = $4,000; one-time total $8,500. MRR carried in: $1,400. New MRR: 5 × $150 + $500 + $400 = $1,650. If new retainers bill from close date, month-3 collections land ~$10.3k; full-month billing shows $11.55k. Either way: **$10k month by day 90 on 22 cumulative deals** — about 2–3 closes per week at full ramp, from ~9 held audits per week. That is not heroic; it's 25–30% close on audits where we show the prospect their own missed calls.

### Why retainers are the whole game

- Month 3 exits with **$3,050 MRR across 14 accounts** (11 receptionist, 2 Local SEO, 1 Marketing Engine). That bills again next month before anyone sells anything.
- Keep adding just ~$1,500 of new MRR/month at 95% retention and MRR compounds to **~$7,900/mo by month 6 and ~$13,800/mo by month 12** — at which point $10k months happen with zero project sales, and every project is upside.
- Unit durability: one $150/mo receptionist client = $1,800/yr for a few config-hours of delivery; margin on the underlying stack is fat (white-label receptionist wholesale runs $25–$99/mo; missed-call-text-back SMS costs ~$0.008/message — RESEARCH_SMB_MARKET §2).
- Referral flywheel stacks on top: a referred client is worth ~$1,080 to the referrer over 5 years under our program (10% of project cost years 1–3, 3% after) — meaning a referred $1,500 project costs us $150 in year-1 payout, cheaper than any paid channel. ## 4. Channel plan

Five channels, each with an owner, a daily number, and a playbook. Volumes below are steady-state (from ~week 4, once email domains are warmed); benchmarks cited from `RESEARCH_OUTREACH.md` / `RESEARCH_SEO.md`.

| Channel | Effort (steady state) | Volume/mo | Benchmark | Expected booked audits/mo | Playbook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold email | 1 owner; 2 × 30-min inbox blocks/day | ~2,000 verified sends (2 domains × 2 mailboxes × 25/day, 20 days) | ~3.4% avg reply (Instantly), 5–10% good; 0.5–1% of sends book a meeting | **10–20** | `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md` |
| Cold calling | 2–3 callers × 40–50 hand dials/day | ~2,000–2,600 dials | Gong (300M calls): ~2.3–2.5% of dials → meeting (~40 dials/meeting) on clean SMB lists; we plan at 50–60% of benchmark for a junior team | **20–30** | `04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md` |
| LinkedIn + social | All 11 profiles optimized; 2 posters; 15 connects/day | ~300 connects, 8–10 posts | Manual-only (ToS-safe); conversion is relationship-paced | **4–8** | `06_LINKEDIN_SOCIAL_PLAYBOOK.md` |
| Referral partners | 1 partnerships owner | 12 past clients + consultant network activated; 5 partner conversations/wk | $1,080/referral 5-yr pitch; ~70% probability a referral exceeds $1,000 payout (internal model) | **2–5 warm intros** (close at 2–3x cold rate) | `07_REFERRAL_ACTIVATION.md` |
| SEO / inbound | 1–2 owners; 1 substantive page or post/wk | Vertical pages + GBP + citations | AI-referral traffic converts ~14.2% vs 2.8% organic (Sapt); long-tail KD<30 only | **0–2 (mo 1) → 3–6 (mo 3)** | `05_SEO_PLAYBOOK.md` |

**Totals at steady state: ~40–60 booked audits/mo → ~30–42 held → 8–12 new clients/mo at 25–30% close.** That covers the Month-3 revenue table with margin.

Channel rules:
- Cold email volume is capped by physics, not ambition: 20–50/day/mailbox, 3-week warmup, <2% bounce (verify every list — MillionVerifier, $37/10k), spam complaints <0.1%. Burn a domain and you lose 3+ weeks. Full rules in `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`.
- Calling is our fastest channel to first revenue (no warmup period) — it carries weeks 1–3 while email domains warm. Hand-dial only; hours and state rules in `04_COLD_CALLING_PLAYBOOK.md`.
- Every inbound lead (site form, GBP, referral) gets a response **within 24 hours as promised publicly — target 5 minutes**, because 5-minute responders are ~21x more likely to qualify the lead (GreetNow). We sell speed-to-lead; we must model it.
- Total outbound tooling budget: **~$80–115/mo** (2 secondary domains + 4 Google Workspace mailboxes $28 + Instantly $37–47 + Apollo free + Hunter free + verification ~$10). Approved; nothing else without founder sign-off.

## 7. Top 5 risks and mitigations

**1. Deliverability burn — a torched sending domain sets email back 3+ weeks.**
Mitigations (enforced in `03_COLD_EMAIL_PLAYBOOK.md`): never send cold from `myconsulting.network`; 2–3 mailboxes/domain max; 3-week warmup before real volume and warmup stays on at 20–40% forever; hard cap 50/day/mailbox, never raise volume >20%/day; verify every list (<2% bounce); one-click unsubscribe headers on; monitor Google Postmaster — complaint rate is enforced at 0.3% and our target is <0.1%, which at our volumes means roughly one complaint per thousand sends is already the alarm. Kill-switch: any mailbox with a bounce spike or complaint >0.1% pauses that day.

**2. Compliance violation — the fines are existential.**
CAN-SPAM: up to $53,088 per email; TCPA: $500–$1,500 per call with a plaintiff's-bar industry behind it; TSR civil penalties $53,088/violation and its fraud rules now cover B2B. Mitigations: physical address + working opt-out in every email (template-locked in `templates/`), opt-outs honored same day (law says 10 business days), hand-dial only (no ATDS/AI voice — SMB numbers are often personal cells, and mixed-use cells keep consumer protections per *Chennette v. Porch*), 8am–9pm prospect-local calling window (8am–8pm and 3-attempts/24h cap in FL/OK), internal do-not-contact list checked before every send/dial, DNC scrub every 31 days for sole-proprietor lists, US-only lists (Canada/EU law differs sharply).

**3. Capacity crunch — selling faster than 11 mostly-junior people can deliver.**
Mitigations: only productized scopes from §2 (pre-solved builds, templates, vendor infrastructure — configuration, not invention); WIP caps set in `10_TEAM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` (e.g., max 2 website builds and 6 receptionist installs in flight); every deal gets a named PM at close; overflow goes to the consultant network at the 75% split rather than being declined; a standing "pause-sell" trigger — if delivery backlog exceeds 2 weeks, calling pod shifts from booking audits to onboarding calls.

**4. AI liability — a client's bot says something wrong and we own the fallout.**
Air Canada was held liable for its own chatbot's incorrect answer (*Moffatt v. Air Canada*, 2024). Hard guardrails, non-negotiable: no unsupervised bot ever states refund/policy terms, prices, or gives medical or legal advice — those intents route to a human, full stop; hard-scoped scripts + disclaimers on every deployment; **no patient/PHI data flows without BAAs and HIPAA-compliant vendors** (healthcare segment is gated on this — see `01_TARGET_SEGMENTS.md`); all client SMS on A2P 10DLC-registered numbers (unregistered SMS at scale = carrier fines/blocking); we decline custom LLM builds, $15K+ bespoke chatbots, and outcome guarantees we can't attribute — that tier belongs to engineering shops, and our terms page already disclaims guaranteed results. These guardrails are also a sales asset: lawyers and dentists buy *because* we lead with them.

**5. Margin/cash-flow erosion — discounting and float quietly kill the model.**
Consultants take 75% of brokered project cost; MyCo nets ~13.9% there, so a "small" discount on brokered work can erase the entire margin, and paying consultants before clients pay creates ~$1,125/project float. Mitigations: absolute floor $400/engagement, target $1,000+; setup fees ($750) collected up front before configuration starts; milestone billing on anything multi-month; retainers billed on the 1st via card/ACH autopay; productized retainers delivered by the internal team (full margin) are the growth engine, brokered custom work is the upsell; discounts >10% require founder sign-off.
